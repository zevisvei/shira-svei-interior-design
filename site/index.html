Four stages, one team on the ground

Buying off-plan happens in stages. Bring us in at any one of them, or have us with you through all four.

1When you get the prospectus

When you get the prospectus

Is this the right apartment for you?

Before you commit, we review the contractor’s sales plan with you. We check real dimensions, furniture clearances, circulation and storage, so you know whether the space fits the way you live.

A spacious living room. Or is it?

The sales plan makes the living and dining area look roomy. Drawn to scale, the dining chair overlaps the loveseat and blocks the way to the hallway. Drag to compare.

Once you’ve chosen the apartment

Make the layout work for you.

We rethink the rooms around your family: room for another bedroom, a more open living area, more built-in storage. We draw the changes and take them to the contractor’s team.

Every contractor limits what can change. We know where the room to move is, and use it.

When selections are due

4Getting ready to move in

Getting ready to move in

Furnish and style your home.

We plan and source furniture room by room, and design built-ins where they use the space better: a vanity fitted to the bathroom, storage set into a niche, a piece sized to keep a walkway clear. Then curtains, rugs, lighting, art and the last details.

Our own local tradespeople and suppliers build and deliver it.
