How a two-bedroom apartment became a three-bedroom home

The original plan had two bedrooms and a separate kitchen. We turned the kitchen into a third bedroom, then fitted a compact kitchen into the old laundry area and part of the living room entrance. The family gained a room, and the living and dining area stayed open.
