Design packages

Help with one decision, or one team with you from the first floor plan to the last cushion.

Prospectus review

For buyers deciding whether a new-build apartment suits them, before they commit.

Ask about a prospectus review
  • We go through the contractor’s sales plan and dimensions with you in English.
  • We check furniture clearances, circulation, storage and how the layout fits your day.
  • We point out the changes worth raising with the contractor.

Tiles, bathrooms and kitchen

Your kitchen and bathroom decisions with one team, in person in Israel or remotely.

Ask about selections
  • Floor and wall tiles chosen together with sinks, toilets, tubs and shower fittings.
  • A kitchen planned around how you cook and host: layout, appliances, counters and storage.
  • We work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier or an independent kitchen company, review the design with you and flag what to change.

Furnishing and styling

For buyers who want the apartment furnished and ready to live in.

Ask about furnishing
  • Furniture planned and sourced for each room, with custom cabinetry where the space calls for it.
  • Our own local tradespeople and suppliers make and install the custom pieces.
  • Rugs, curtains, lighting, art and accessories chosen to work together.
  • The apartment styled so it feels finished when you arrive.

Full-service support

One team on the ground in Israel, guiding you in English from the first floor plan through furnishing.

Ask about full-service support
  • We start with a plan review and set priorities and deadlines together.
  • Layout changes, kitchen design, tile selection and every decision in between. We can also furnish and style the apartment.
  • We speak with the contractor and suppliers for you, and follow up as questions come in.

Questions buyers ask us

When should we contact you?

While you’re reviewing the sales plan, or after you’ve signed while the contractor can still make changes. The earlier we start, the more options are still open.

Can you help if we live in the U.S.?

Yes. We work in English, go through the plans with you remotely, and speak with the contractor’s team in Israel on your behalf. Tile selection can be done in person or remotely.

Do you speak with the contractor for us?

Yes. When a revised drawing arrives, a supplier has a question or the contractor needs a decision, we explain it to you in English and follow up with the team in Israel.

What can actually be changed?

Every contractor sets its own limits and deadlines. We know where the flexibility usually is, and use it to fit as many details as possible to the way you live.

Can you work with the contractor’s kitchen supplier?

Yes. We can work with the contractor’s supplier or an independent kitchen company, review the design with you and flag what to change.

Can you furnish the whole apartment?

Yes. We plan and source furniture room by room, design custom cabinetry made by our local tradespeople, and style the apartment so it’s ready when you arrive.

What should we send first?

The sales plan or latest plan you received, the developer’s list of standard finishes if you have it, any deadline for changes, and what you want to work better.
