From sales plan to building plans

We work with buyers purchasing apartments on paper from Israeli contractors. Start while you’re still reviewing a sales plan, or after you’ve signed while changes are still possible. Send us the plan and your deadlines, and we’ll go through them together in English.

Where we join you

Four points in an off-plan purchase where a decision is due. Each one closes for good once the contractor moves on.

  1. 1Before you commit

    Review the prospectus

    We check the dimensions and draw furniture to scale, so you can see how the rooms, storage and clearances work before you choose the apartment.

  2. 2After you’ve chosen

    Adapt the layout

    We explore changes that fit the apartment to you, prepare the drawings and bring your requests to the contractor’s team.

  3. 3When selections are due

    Choose the details

    Tiles, kitchen design, air conditioning, doors and fixtures, with views of how your choices look in your own layout.

  4. 4Getting ready to move in

    Furnish and style

    We plan the furniture, source the pieces and add textiles, lighting, art and accessories, so the apartment is ready to live in.

At every stage we explain the choices and deadlines in English, and act as your contact with the Israeli project team.

Downsizing from the U.S.: where will everything go?

In an Israeli apartment, coats, bulk groceries, luggage and seasonal things all need a place inside the living space. We look at what you use every day and where it makes sense to keep it. Sometimes moving a doorway frees a whole wall for storage and keeps the room easy to walk through.

What you had in the U.S.

  • A coat closet
  • A walk-in pantry
  • A basement or garage
  • A laundry room

What we plan in its place

  • A shallow cabinet near the entry
  • Full-height pantry storage
  • Wardrobes set into a niche
  • Cabinets above the washer and dryer

What are you buying for?

The answer changes which decisions matter now and which can wait.

Moving in at handover

Plan the kitchen, storage, lighting and rooms around how your family lives today, so the apartment works from the day you get the keys.

A home for later

Use the construction stage for the decisions that are hard to undo later, like walls, plumbing and electrical points, and keep the rest flexible.

An investment

Weigh the contractor’s standard options against paid upgrades. For a rental, favor a practical layout, durable finishes and easy upkeep.

What to send us from the U.S.

Tell us what you want to work better, whether that’s seating for Shabbos meals, kitchen storage or a bathroom layout.

Tell us about your apartment
  • The sales plan, or the latest plan you received
  • The developer’s list of standard finishes, if you have it
  • Any deadline for submitting changes
  • What you want to work better in the apartment
