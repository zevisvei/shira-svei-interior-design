# Product

## The business

Shira Svei Interior Design is a studio in Israel. It works with buyers who purchase apartments "on paper" from Israeli contractors, and acts as their design team and point of contact on the ground.

## Audience

American buyers (and other English speakers abroad) buying a new-build apartment in Israel. They can't be on site, don't know how Israeli contractors work, and face deadlines for plan changes and selections. Many are downsizing from a U.S. house; some buy to move in at handover, some for later, some as an investment.

## What the site must do

One job: get a buyer to tell the studio about their apartment (the contact page). Every page ends by leading there.

## Services

Four stages of an off-plan purchase, which are a real sequence:

1. **Prospectus review**: check the sales plan's real dimensions and furniture clearances before the buyer commits.
2. **Layout changes**: adapt rooms (open a bedroom, add a third bedroom, more storage) and take requests to the contractor.
3. **Selections**: tiles, sanitaryware, kitchen design, air conditioning, doors, fixtures, with photorealistic renderings.
4. **Furnishing and styling**: furniture, custom cabinetry through local tradespeople, textiles, lighting, art.

Packages: Prospectus review, Tiles/bathrooms/kitchen, Furnishing and styling, Full-service support. Contact page package query values: `plan-review`, `selections`, `furnishing`, `ongoing-support`.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Speak to the buyer as "you", the studio as "we". Name concrete things (sales plan, clearances, Shabbos meals, coat closet) rather than selling. No superlatives, no invented proof, no exclamation marks. Buttons say what happens ("Tell us about your apartment").
